# SentinelAI — Public Safety Perception Survey

Ready to paste into Google Forms. Target: 20–50 responses from students, women
commuters, and daily travellers. Takes respondents ~3 minutes.

**Form settings:** collect no names or emails (anonymous = honest answers);
enable "response summary" charts — screenshots of those charts become your
validation evidence attachment.

---

**Form title:** Public Safety on Roads & Streets — 3-minute survey

**Description:** We are a student team researching how safe people feel in
public spaces and how emergencies get reported. Anonymous, ~3 minutes.

### Section 1 — About you

1. Age group — *Multiple choice:* Under 18 / 18–24 / 25–40 / 41–60 / 60+
2. Gender — *Multiple choice:* Woman / Man / Prefer not to say / Other
3. How do you usually travel after dark? — *Checkboxes:* Walk / Two-wheeler /
   Car / Bus or metro / Auto or cab / I avoid going out after dark

### Section 2 — Safety experience

4. How safe do you feel walking alone on your usual routes after dark?
   — *Linear scale 1 (very unsafe) – 5 (very safe)*
5. Have you ever avoided a road, shortcut or area because it felt unsafe or
   poorly lit? — *Multiple choice:* Often / Sometimes / Rarely / Never
6. Have you or someone close to you ever witnessed a road accident?
   — *Multiple choice:* Yes, and help arrived quickly / Yes, and help was
   delayed / Yes, and no help came until much later / No
7. In an emergency on the street, what would you actually do first?
   — *Multiple choice:* Call 112/100 / Call family / Use an SOS app /
   Shout for help from people nearby / I'm not sure

### Section 3 — Reaction to the idea

*(Show this text before Q8):* "Imagine streetlights that automatically detect
accidents or distress (a crash sound, a scream, an SOS button press), verify
the emergency with multiple sensors, and alert police/ambulance with the exact
location — while brightening the street and recording evidence."

8. How useful would this be on the routes you use?
   — *Linear scale 1 (not useful) – 5 (extremely useful)*
9. Would this make you more willing to walk or travel after dark?
   — *Multiple choice:* Yes, definitely / Somewhat / No difference / No
10. What would concern you most about such a system? — *Checkboxes:*
    Privacy / False alarms / Whether police actually respond /
    Cost to the city / Cameras being misused / Nothing — I'd want it
11. Where should it be installed first? — *Checkboxes:* Highways & accident
    black-spots / Isolated streets near colleges & hostels / Bus stops &
    metro exits / Parks & markets / Residential lanes
12. Anything you'd like to tell us about feeling safe (or unsafe) in public
    spaces? — *Paragraph, optional*

---

## After collecting responses

1. Screenshot the Google Forms summary charts for Q4, Q6, Q8, Q9, Q10.
2. Save as `validation/survey-results.pdf` and add one paragraph of takeaways.
3. Quote the strongest numbers on deck slide 2 or 13 (e.g. "84% of 42
   respondents said they'd feel safer after dark") — real numbers from even a
   small sample beat any market-report citation.
