SentinelAI

Transforming Every Streetlight into a Silent Guardian
AI-Driven Distributed Public Safety Mesh for Smart Cities — Concept Document
Concept author: Nirisha Chowdary  ·  Team SentinelAI (Saatwik · Nirisha Chowdary)  ·  July 2026

1. Vision & Mission

Vision. Build an AI-powered public safety infrastructure that transforms existing streetlights and public spaces into intelligent emergency response systems — reducing emergency response time, improving women's safety, and saving lives.

Mission. Leverage Artificial Intelligence, Edge Computing and IoT to create a scalable, affordable, privacy-conscious public safety platform for India, and eventually the world.

2. Problem Statement

Every year, thousands of people lose their lives in road accidents because emergency response arrives too late. Women and vulnerable individuals face safety threats in isolated public spaces where immediate help is unavailable. Current solutions fall short:

  • CCTV cameras only record incidents — nobody responds in real time.
  • Emergency apps require a conscious victim to manually request help.
  • Public infrastructure is passive rather than intelligent.
  • Emergency services receive delayed, incomplete, unverified information.

India needs a system that can detect, verify and respond to emergencies automatically.

3. Solution

SentinelAI transforms existing streetlights, bus stops and public infrastructure into AI-powered emergency response nodes. Each smart node continuously monitors its surroundings using on-device AI and multiple sensors. Instead of waiting for someone to report an incident, SentinelAI automatically detects accidents and distress situations, verifies emergencies through multi-sensor fusion, alerts authorities and family with exact GPS location, guides emergency responders along the safest route, and records evidence securely.

4. Core Features

AI Accident Detection

Detects vehicle, bike and pedestrian accidents by fusing the AI camera, microphone and vibration sensor.

Women's Safety

Triggered by an SOS press, a scream, or AI-detected suspicious activity. SentinelAI alerts police and family, increases street lighting, activates nearby cameras, and records evidence.

Safe Corridor

Nearby smart poles communicate as a network. During an emergency, poles along the route brighten, cameras hand off tracking, responders receive the safest route, and units know exactly where to go.

Edge AI

Data is processed locally on the pole instead of streaming to the cloud — faster response, lower bandwidth, better privacy.

Predictive AI

Beyond detection, SentinelAI predicts accident-prone roads, unsafe locations, repeated crime hotspots and infrastructure failures.

6. System Architecture

Sensors (camera · microphone · vibration · GPS · SOS button)  →  Edge AI Processor  →  Emergency Validation Engine  →  Response fan-out: Police · Ambulance · Family · Cloud Dashboard · Smart Streetlights

7. User Flows

Accident. Vehicle crash → AI detects impact → microphone confirms sound → vibration confirms collision → AI verifies emergency → GPS locks location → police notified → ambulance notified → streetlights brighten → evidence stored.
Women's safety. SOS pressed → AI activates → nearby cameras start recording → emergency lights ON → police receive live location → family notified.

10. Innovation

Unlike traditional CCTV, SentinelAI detects, understands, verifies and responds instead of simply recording. The Safe Corridor feature lets multiple smart poles coordinate an emergency response as a network rather than acting independently.

Patent opportunities

  • Multi-sensor emergency validation algorithm
  • Distributed AI communication between smart poles
  • AI-driven Safe Corridor activation
  • Edge-based emergency detection framework

Patentability depends on detailed prior-art searches and the specific technical implementation — a formal search is a Phase 1 deliverable.

12. Risks & Safeguards

Privacy concerns, false alarms, weather effects on sensors, hardware maintenance, government approvals and installation costs are addressed through privacy-by-design (edge-first processing), multi-sensor fusion, local AI, maintenance planning, and partnerships with public authorities.

15. Why SentinelAI Matters

SentinelAI isn't another surveillance system. It makes public infrastructure aware, responsive and connected — enabling faster emergency response and safer public spaces. By combining AI, edge computing and IoT with infrastructure cities already own, it seeks to reduce preventable deaths and support India's vision of smarter, more resilient cities.
